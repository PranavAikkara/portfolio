About — the short version

Pranav P.
I make AI dependable.

Associate Data Scientist at FarmwiseAI. I live in the gap between a clever model and a service people trust.

I came up through data science and kept following the problems downstream, into retrieval, into voice, into the deeply unglamorous question of why the pipeline fell over at 3am. I like that question. The answer is always specific, and the fix always teaches me something about a system I thought I understood.

My favourite part of the work is the part most people skip: the second ninety percent. Anyone can make a model look impressive in a notebook. The interesting engineering starts the moment it has to handle a caller it didn't expect, a document it can't read, or a latency budget that won't forgive a slow path.
