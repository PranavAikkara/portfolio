Colophon — the masthead

I'm Pranav. I turn
promising models into
systems you can trust.

Associate Data Scientist at FarmwiseAI, where the gap between a clever notebook and a service real people depend on is exactly my job.

My favourite part of the work is the part most people skip: the second 90%.

Anyone can get a model to look impressive in a demo. The interesting engineering starts the moment it has to handle a caller it didn't expect, a document it can't read, a latency budget that won't forgive a slow path. That's the territory I work in.

I came up through data science and kept following the problems downstream, into retrieval, into voice, into the deeply unglamorous question of why the pipeline fell over at 3am. I like that question. The answer is always specific, and the fix always teaches you something about the system you thought you understood.

I write things down as I go. This site is partly a portfolio and partly that habit, made public.

Method

A few things I believe
about building this stuff.

i.

Traceability over magic

If you can't point at where an answer came from, you don't have a feature, you have a liability.

ii.

Evals before vibes

A test you trust is worth more than a demo that dazzles. Build the ruler first.

iii.

Latency is a feature

Especially in voice. Speed isn't polish, it's whether the thing is usable at all.

iv.

Small models, narrow jobs

Don't pay frontier prices for a task a fine-tuned small model nails.

v.

Fix the data first

Half of what looks like a model problem is a structure problem wearing a costume.

vi.

Write it down

The system you can't explain is the system you'll be paged about.
